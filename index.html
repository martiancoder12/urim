03 · The method

Designed to be reviewed, not believed.

The product should make its reasoning inspectable at the same level as its conclusion. Every layer has a named input, a bounded output, and a human responsibility.

01

Matter record

Posture, decision, known facts, missing evidence, and declared assumptions.

Human confirms scope
02

Comparator set

Relevant decided matters, inclusion logic, exclusions, and source provenance.

Counsel can challenge fit
03

Specialist panel

Bounded analytical models contribute estimates, uncertainty, and dissent.

Agreement is not concealed
04

Calibrated range

A distribution and sensitivity view, not a categorical forecast.

False precision is rejected
05

Critical path

The ordered events that strengthen, weaken, or break the current thesis.

Counsel owns the decision

04 · Reviewability

Every conclusion carries its file.

URIM is intended to make the basis of a recommendation portable: into the partner meeting, client call, claims file, or investment committee.

Sources

Authorities and comparator records linked to the proposition they support.

Assumptions

Material assumptions separated from observed facts and marked for review.

Sensitivity

The events with enough leverage to change the current recommendation.

Dissent

Material disagreement preserved instead of averaged into invisibility.

Version

Model, data, analyst review, and revision reason recorded together.

05 · Evidence standard

No performance theatre.

Public validation will be dated, versioned, scoped to a matter class and jurisdiction, and linked to method. Until that exists, URIM will not publish a corpus count, accuracy claim, win-rate claim, or simulated live feed as evidence.

  • Temporal testingEvaluate on decisions the system could not have seen.
  • Calibration firstTest whether stated confidence corresponds to observed outcomes.
  • Failure inventoryPublish exclusions, abstentions, missing data, and material failure modes.
  • Change recordRecord what changed, when, why, and which briefs may be affected.
