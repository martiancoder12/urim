# URIM working instructions

Before doing project work, read `README.md` completely. It is the strategic
source of truth for the product, market, evidence standard, brand, interface,
research program, and roadmap.

## Session protocol

1. Identify the current research phase and buyer decision.
2. Inspect existing work and preserve unrelated or user-authored changes.
3. Separate verified facts, product intent, illustrative specimens, and open
   assumptions.
4. Apply the **60% investment memorandum / 25% judicial reasons / 15%
   analytical instrument** model.
5. Apply all three URIM tests:
   - Can a senior decision-maker understand it in five minutes?
   - Can counsel inspect and challenge every material assumption?
   - Could it enter a client, claims, or investment file without apology?
6. Protect confidentiality, privilege, provenance, human judgment, and model
   limitations.
7. Never invent performance, corpus size, customers, partnerships, coverage,
   validation, scarcity, or response times.
8. Keep the initial product narrow without obstructing later portfolio use.
9. Verify work in proportion to legal, financial, privacy, and reputational
   risk.
10. Update `README.md` when the owner makes a durable strategic decision.

## Non-negotiable interface constraints

- Present URIM as decision intelligence for complex disputes, not a general
  chatbot or autonomous legal adviser.
- Begin with the decision and reviewable output, not model spectacle.
- Make illustrative content unmistakable and keep it separate from evidence.
- Use calibrated ranges, critical paths, sensitivities, assumptions, and
  versions instead of theatrical certainty.
- Default to paper, ink, docket blue, and selective redline.
- Do not reintroduce Matrix rain, CRT effects, neon-green identity, fake live
  feeds, boot sequences, magnetic controls, or scroll hijacking.
- Motion must explain state, evidence, or scenario change and must honor reduced
  motion.
- The public form must never ask for party names, documents, confidential facts,
  or privileged strategy.
